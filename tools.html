Free Fitness Tools

Simple calculators for adults that turn a few numbers into clear, practical next steps. They run in your browser, take about a minute, and come with plain-language guidance from Sanjeev Kumar. They give estimates for education, not medical advice.

BMI Calculator

Check your adult body mass index in metric or imperial, with an age-aware chart and South Asian cut-offs.

Open calculator

BMR Calculator

Estimate your basal metabolic rate and daily calories, with three simple graphs and gentle starting ranges.

Open calculator

How to use these tools together

Start with the BMI calculator. It gives you a quick screening number from your height and weight and shows where you sit on an age-aware chart. Then open the BMR and daily calorie calculator: it fills in your age, height and weight from the BMI page in the same tab, adds your sex, activity level and goal, and estimates the calories your body uses at rest and across a normal day. Together they give you a starting point for training and eating, not a verdict. Neither one can see muscle, health history or medication, so talk to your doctor about anything that worries you.

Built by a local personal trainer

Sanjeev Kumar is a Level 3 personal trainer based in South Surrey who works with adults across Surrey, White Rock, Langley and Vancouver, in person and online. If your numbers raise questions about where to begin, a short conversation often helps more than another calculation.
