Your Body Isn’t Like Everyone Else’s. Your Training Shouldn’t Be Either.

Training guidance starts from your age, lifestyle, movement patterns, previous experience, goals and challenges. Cookie-cutter advice skips that. I start with your story - then share guidance that actually fits the life you’re living.
