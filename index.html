His Story

From Being Bullied at 12 to Becoming a Certified Coach.

  1. Age 12

    A Turning Point

    Bullied for being overweight, Sanjeev made a decision to change - his father steered him toward cricket instead of the gym, and that choice changed everything.

  2. Youth

    Cricket & Self-Study

    His cricket coach introduced him to mobility, nutrition and performance training. He lost 10 kg, and began studying fitness on his own out of genuine curiosity.

  3. Age 16

    His Mother's Diagnosis

    His mother was diagnosed with Stage 4 ovarian cancer. Determined to help, he threw himself into learning about nutrition and lifestyle change - an experience that reshaped how he thinks about health.

  4. College

    Training Through College

    While completing his B.Tech, he kept training in the gym and started coaching friends - even as he pursued a career in electronics.

  5. 2021

    Becoming a Trainer

    Realizing his passion wasn't in his engineering job, Sanjeev became a professional personal trainer in Mohali, Punjab.

  6. 2022

    Fitness Manager

    Promoted to Fitness Manager at Pro Ultimate Gyms, Chandigarh, deepening his leadership and coaching experience.

  7. 2024

    A New Chapter in Canada

    Immigrated to Canada and pursued Canadian-recognized certifications through ISSA - now holding 12+ certifications and counting.

  8. Today

    How You Live

    Sanjeev believes fitness isn't about changing how you look - it's about changing how you live. That belief now shapes every client he coaches.
