Meet Sanjeev

Fitness Is More Than How You Look In the Mirror.

I’m Sanjeev Kumar, a Level 3 personal trainer based in Vancouver and South Surrey. I started as a Level 1 coach, earned my way to Level 3, and have served as Manager on Duty at Fitness World - learning how to lead people, not just write workouts.

I’m NASM-certified and keep studying because bodies, hormones, joints and lives change. My aim isn’t to shout reps at you. It’s to share guidance so you understand your starting point, move with purpose, and feel capable in the body you live in every day.

His Story

From Being Bullied at 12 to Becoming a Certified Coach.

  1. Age 12

    A Turning Point

    Bullied for being overweight, Sanjeev made a decision to change - his father steered him toward cricket instead of the gym, and that choice changed everything.

  2. Youth

    Cricket & Self-Study

    His cricket coach introduced him to mobility, nutrition and performance training. He lost 10 kg, and began studying fitness on his own out of genuine curiosity.

  3. Age 16

    His Mother's Diagnosis

    His mother was diagnosed with Stage 4 ovarian cancer. Determined to help, he threw himself into learning about nutrition and lifestyle change - an experience that reshaped how he thinks about health.

  4. College

    Training Through College

    While completing his B.Tech, he kept training in the gym and started coaching friends - even as he pursued a career in electronics.

  5. 2021

    Becoming a Trainer

    Realizing his passion wasn't in his engineering job, Sanjeev became a professional personal trainer in Mohali, Punjab.

  6. 2022

    Fitness Manager

    Promoted to Fitness Manager at Pro Ultimate Gyms, Chandigarh, deepening his leadership and coaching experience.

  7. 2024

    A New Chapter in Canada

    Immigrated to Canada and pursued Canadian-recognized certifications through NASM and ISSA - now holding 12+ certifications and counting.

  8. Today

    How You Live

    Sanjeev believes fitness isn't about changing how you look - it's about changing how you live. That belief now shapes every client he coaches.

The Sanjeev Method

A Smarter Approach to Fitness Starts With Understanding You.

  1. 01

    Understand - Start With You

    We talk through your history, movement, lifestyle and goals before any advice is shared.

  2. 02

    Build - Build Your Foundation

    We cover the basics: mobility, form, confidence and habits you can actually follow.

  3. 03

    Progress - Progress With Purpose

    Load, skill and capacity increase only when your body is ready - not on a generic calendar.

  4. 04

    Sustain - Make Fitness Part of Life

    The aim is independence: knowledge and strength that last long after a conversation ends.
