1. Who I am

This website, getfitwithsanjeev.com, is run by Sanjeev Kumar. The business address is 1715 152 St #120, Surrey, BC V4A 4N3, Canada.

When this policy says "I", it means Sanjeev Kumar. I collect as little personal information as needed.

2. Adults only

This website is intended for adults aged 18 and older. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before using this website. If I learn that I have collected information from someone under 18, I will delete it.

3. What I collect

I may collect personal information in the following ways:

  • Contact form - name, email, phone (optional) and message.
  • Feedback form - message and optional contact information.
  • BMI calculator form - name, email, phone (optional) and message - and only if you choose to share your results: age group, BMI value, BMI category and unit preference.
  • Cal.com booking - name, email, time zone, appointment time and any questions you include.
  • Email correspondence - any information you include in emails you send me.
  • Client relationship - if you later become a client, I may collect goals, health history and health information under a separate agreement.
  • Automatically through hosting - IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited.

4. BMI calculator

The age, height and weight you enter are used only in your browser. To keep your result visible while you move around the site, the page keeps them in your browser's temporary session storage on your device. They are deleted when you close the tab or press 'Clear my results', and they are never sent to me unless you choose to share them through the contact form. They are not saved in cookies or included in the URL.

The calculator is a screening tool and is not medical advice.

5. Why I collect your information

I use the information I collect to:

  • Reply to your messages
  • Schedule consultations
  • Keep records of client agreements
  • Maintain the security of this website
  • Comply with legal obligations

I do not sell your personal information. I will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

6. Consent

By sending a message, using the BMI calculator with the share option, or booking a consultation, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time - see section 12.

7. Who else may access your information

I use the following third-party services to operate this website:

  • Formspree - to receive form submissions.
  • Cal.com and Google (Calendar and Gmail) - to manage bookings and communication.
  • Vercel - to host this website.

These services may process your information outside Canada, including in the United States and Europe.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. There is no analytics service currently installed. Cal.com may set its own cookies when you use the booking calendar.

I will update this policy before adding any analytics service.

9. Photos and testimonials

I will only use your photo or testimonial with your written permission. Faces will be blurred unless you agree otherwise. You can ask me to remove your photo or testimonial at any time.

10. Retention

I keep personal information for up to 24 months unless you are an active client or the law requires a longer retention period. After that, I delete or anonymize the information.

11. Security

I take reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information. However, I cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

You have the right to see, correct, withdraw consent for or delete the personal information I hold about you. To make a request, email getfitwithsk007@gmail.com. I will normally respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia at oipc.bc.ca.

13. Links to other websites

This website contains links to LinkedIn and Instagram. I am not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
